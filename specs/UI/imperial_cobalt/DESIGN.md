## Brand & Style

This design system translates the timeless precision and structural purity of classical Qinghua porcelain into a modern, mission-critical enterprise design language. It embodies intellectual clarity, enduring prestige, and architectural rigor.

### Personality & Tone
- **Luminous & Pristine:** Surfaces evoke hand-thrown, twice-fired kaolin porcelain—flawlessly white, smooth, and crystalline.
- **Architectural & Authoritative:** High-density enterprise tooling balanced by balanced calligraphic harmony and strict structural grids.
- **Crafted & Deliberate:** Crisp crackle-glazed separations and pigmented cobalt blues eliminate visual noise, creating an atmosphere of composed control.

### Design Movement
**Neoclassical Minimalist Enterprise:** A fusion of high-precision enterprise data density and Chinese ceramic restraint. The system rejects gratuitous blur and skeuomorphism in favor of pure ceramic surfaces, hairline porcelain fissures (fine neutral borders), and saturated cobalt pigment layers.

## Colors

The palette is derived directly from classical cobalt oxide pigment firing over pristine white kaolin clay.

### Core Swatches
- **Primary (Cobalt Glaze - `#1A4B8C`):** The quintessential deep blue pigment. Used for key interactive states, primary actions, active navigational indicators, and primary visual emphasis. Extended cobalt scales span `#1E40AF` (hover state) to `#2563EB` (selection highlighting).
- **Secondary (Celadon Accent - `#0D9488`):** A muted jade-green glaze inspired by Longquan celadon. Reserved for operational stability markers, positive verification, tags, and system health status.
- **Tertiary (Cinnabar Seal - `#BE123C`):** An imperial vermilion/cinnabar seal ink. Applied sparingly to critical alerts, security indicators, error boundaries, and priority audit badges.
- **Neutral (Imperial Indigo - `#0F172A`):** Deep indigo-tinted black derived from traditional mineral ink blocks, anchoring typography and icons without the harshness of pure black (`#000000`).

### Porcelain Surface Hierarchy
- **Canvas (`#F1F5F9`):** The structural bisque clay background; grounds views with subtle cool warmth.
- **Surface Level 1 (`#F8FAFC`):** First glaze layer; sidebars, structural rails, and inactive panes.
- **Surface Level 2 (`#FFFFFF`):** High-fire glaze; data cards, active tables, modals, and content viewports.
- **Crackle Line Borders (`#E2E8F0` & `#CBD5E1`):** Fine ceramic hairline borders defining component separation.

## Typography

The typographic hierarchy combines the academic authority of imperial publishing with the clinical legibility required by high-velocity enterprise software.

### Type Pairings
- **Headlines (`Noto Serif`):** Delivers classical gravitas and editorial presence. Its balanced brackets and stroke contrast echo traditional calligraphy brushes and stone inscriptions.
- **Body & UI Controls (`Hanken Grotesk`):** Highly disciplined, modern grotesque that maintains exceptional clarity at 12px to 16px across high-density data tables and administrative forms.
- **System Telemetry & Data Tables (`JetBrains Mono`):** Engineered for numeric alignment, transaction IDs, log viewing, and micro-labels.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is built upon an architectural grid informed by Chinese courtyard symmetry, enforcing proportion, rhythm, and clear vertical flow.

### Grid System
- **Desktop (≥ 1280px):** 12-column fluid grid, `2rem` (32px) margins, `1.5rem` (24px) gutters. Left persistent navigation rail fixed at 260px or 72px (collapsed).
- **Tablet (768px – 1279px):** 8-column fluid grid, `1.5rem` (24px) margins, `1rem` (16px) gutters. Navigation rail transitions to an off-canvas drawer.
- **Mobile (≤ 767px):** 4-column fluid grid, `1rem` (16px) margins, `0.75rem` (12px) gutters.

### Enterprise Spacing Cadence
- Content padding relies strictly on the `space-*` scale.
- High-density data tables and dense tree views utilize `space-xs` (4px) and `space-sm` (8px) row gaps.
- Administrative containers, master-detail sections, and modal cards utilize `space-md` (16px) and `space-lg` (24px).

## Elevation & Depth

To sustain the pure, vitreous character of glazed porcelain, elevation avoids heavy, muddy drop shadows. Depth is achieved through layered porcelain luminosity, sharp crackle borders, and ultra-sheer cobalt-tinted ambient glows.

### Elevation Hierarchy
- **Level 0 (The Raw Clay Base):** Background canvas (`#F1F5F9`). Completely flat.
- **Level 1 (Glazed Plate):** Workspaces and data grids (`#FFFFFF`). Defined by a single 1px hairline border in `#E2E8F0` with no ambient shadow.
- **Level 2 (Raised Vessels / Dropdowns / Cards):** `#FFFFFF` surface bounded by a 1px border in `#CBD5E1`, supported by a crystalline ambient shadow: `0px 2px 8px -2px rgba(26, 75, 140, 0.06), 0px 1px 3px 0px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.04)`.
- **Level 3 (Floating Overlays / Modals / Drawers):** `#FFFFFF` surface bounded by a 1px border in `#1A4B8C` (15% opacity), lifted by an expanded shadow: `0px 12px 32px -4px rgba(26, 75, 140, 0.12), 0px 4px 12px -2px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.05)`.
- **Level 4 (Toasts / Popovers):** `#FFFFFF` surface bounded by a 1px border in `#1A4B8C` (30% opacity), accompanied by an intense localized accent shadow: `0px 16px 40px -6px rgba(26, 75, 140, 0.18)`.

## Shapes

The shape vocabulary mirrors the subtle softening found on the hand-finished rims of imperial porcelain vessels. Sharp corners are softened just enough to prevent visual harshness without degrading enterprise structural density.

### Geometry Specifications
- **Level 1 Soft (0.25rem / 4px):** Standard across all form controls, chips, table cell selectors, nested panels, and buttons.
- **Large Components (0.5rem / 8px):** Applied to top-level cards, modular dashboards, and dialog containers (`rounded-lg`).
- **Surface Panels (0.75rem / 12px):** Applied exclusively to full-screen drawers and enterprise modal window shells (`rounded-xl`).
- **Pills (Full Radius):** Reserved exclusively for status indicators, micro-counters, and filter tags.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary:** Background `#1A4B8C`, text `#FFFFFF`, border `1px solid #163E75`. Hover transitions to `#1E40AF` with a subtle porcelain glow (`0 0 0 2px rgba(37, 99, 235, 0.2)`). Focus ring: `2px solid #2563EB` offset by 2px white space.
- **Secondary:** Background `#FFFFFF`, text `#1A4B8C`, border `1px solid #CBD5E1`. Hover: background `#F8FAFC`, border `#1A4B8C`.
- **Ghost / Tertiary:** Background transparent, text `#1E293B`. Hover: background `#F1F5F9`, text `#1A4B8C`.
- **Destructive:** Background `#FFFFFF`, text `#BE123C`, border `1px solid #FECDD3`. Hover: background `#FFF1F2`, border `#BE123C`.

### Chips & Badges
- **Status Badges:** Compact 20px height, uppercase `JetBrains Mono` 11px font.
  - *Normal/System:* Background `#F1F5F9`, text `#334155`, border `1px solid #CBD5E1`.
  - *Celadon (Success/Active):* Background `#F0FDFA`, text `#0F766E`, border `1px solid #99F6E4`.
  - *Cinnabar (Critical/Alert):* Background `#FFF1F2`, text `#BE123C`, border `1px solid #FECDD3`.
  - *Imperial Cobalt (Selected/Current):* Background `#EFF6FF`, text `#1A4B8C`, border `1px solid #BFDBFE`.

### Input Fields & Selects
- Base surface `#FFFFFF`, border `1px solid #CBD5E1`, corner radius `4px`, internal padding `8px 12px`.
- Text color `#0F172A`, placeholder color `#94A3B8`.
- Focus state: border color `#1A4B8C`, inner halo `0 0 0 1px #1A4B8C`.
- Error state: border color `#BE123C`, inner halo `0 0 0 1px #BE123C`.

### Checkboxes & Radio Buttons
- Default: `16px x 16px`, background `#FFFFFF`, border `1.5px solid #94A3B8`, radius `3px` (radio: `50%`).
- Checked: background `#1A4B8C`, border `1.5px solid #1A4B8C`, white porcelain checkmark glyph or circular core.
- Indeterminate: background `#1A4B8C`, border `1.5px solid #1A4B8C`, white horizontal bar.

### Cards & Container Panels
- Base background `#FFFFFF`, 1px solid hairline border `#E2E8F0`, radius `8px`.
- Header separator: hairline border `#E2E8F0`, padding `16px 20px`. Card title set in `Noto Serif` 16px medium.

### Data Tables
- Header row: background `#F8FAFC`, bottom border `2px solid #CBD5E1`, text uppercase 12px `Hanken Grotesk` font, color `#475569`.
- Row cells: background `#FFFFFF`, bottom border `1px solid #F1F5F9`, height `40px` (dense) or `52px` (standard).
- Row hover: `#F8FAFC` transition. Row selected: `#EFF6FF` with a left indicator border in `3px solid #1A4B8C`.
